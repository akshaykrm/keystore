## Workspace

---

- Workspace is created by a user and becomes the default owner by membership
- Owner can add other users to workspace
- Workspace ownership can be transferred

---

### Responsibilities

- Owns business data
- Manage business data
- Owner can manage permission for members(later stages)

---

### Open Questions

- Can an workspace be deleted?
- What happens if ownership changes?
- Who will get the ownership if the owner suddenly deletes their account?
- Can a workspace be archived?
